Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

This website

This website has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no ads. It is served by GitHub Pages, which receives your IP address to deliver the pages, as any website host does.

Videos

Videos are not loaded until you tap one. When you do, the video plays from YouTube (youtube-nocookie.com), and Google's privacy policy applies to that video.

Email

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply, and delete them when the conversation is over.

Our games

Each game has its own privacy policy, linked below. In short: no accounts and no servers of our own, your progress stays on your device, some games send anonymous usage statistics and crash reports through Google Firebase, ads come from Google AdMob (a banner and videos you choose to watch), and purchases are handled by Apple or Google.
