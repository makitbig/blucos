FAQ

How do I play?

Swap two neighbouring petals to line up three or more of the same kind. Each bed has goals at the top: collect petals, clear weeds and brambles, or melt frost, within the moves you have. Four in a row makes a breeze that sweeps a row or column, an L or T makes a bee, and five in a row makes a butterfly.

How do flowers grow?

Each flower grows over three beds: a small plant, a bigger one, then full bloom. Bloomed flowers go to My Garden with their name and old meaning, and you can send any of them as a card with your own words.

How do I get coins and items?

Coins come from finishing beds (more the first time, plus a few for moves left) and from watering your garden once a day. Tap your coins at the top to open the shop for +5 moves, Shuffle and the Trowel (up to 5 of each). When you're out, you can watch an optional video for one. Coins and items can't be bought with money.

What is in Petal Garden Pro?

A one-time purchase that removes the banner ad, doubles your coins, gives you one of each item every day and opens the four Pro gardens: Desert Bloom, Tropical Garden, Alpine Meadow and East Asian Garden. You can also open the Pro gardens one by one with coins earned by playing.

I bought Pro on a new phone. How do I get it back?

Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account, open the menu and tap Restore purchase.

Will I lose my garden if I delete the app?

Your garden and progress are saved only on your device, so deleting the app removes them.

Does the game need the internet?

No. Everything works offline. Ads and purchases need a connection.
