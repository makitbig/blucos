Our story

A child who loves to draw, and a dad who wanted those drawings to move

There are always drawings on our kitchen table. Birds with their wings spread wide, dinosaurs down to their bones, stars nobody has named yet. Our kid draws on any paper within reach, and every drawing comes with a story attached: where this bird lives, what that dinosaur ate, how far away that star must be.

Listening to those stories one evening, Dad began to wonder. What if the drawings could breathe on a screen? What if an egg could crack, a skeleton click together, a star switch on, one at a time?

So the evenings became ours. One of us draws and dreams; the other turns it into code. When the artist says a corner needs more sparkle, the sparkle gets fixed. When the coder asks whether a level is too hard, the artist plays it first. Our first player is always the same small person, and the toughest one to please.

Why real things

A child's questions always point at the real world. Why does the cuckoo leave its eggs in someone else's nest? Was T. rex really that big? Why is the red star in Orion red?

So the birds, the fossils and the stars in our games are all real, and each one comes with a true story. We hope that after the game is closed, you look a little longer at the bird outside the window, the bones in the museum, the sky at night.

Small, slow, with care

We are not a big company. We are a small family studio on Jeju Island, and we make one game at a time.

We make the games our kid actually wants to play, and we leave out anything we would be embarrassed to hand to a child. That is why there are no ads jumping out between levels, and no boxes that ask you to pay for a chance.

About the name

Blucos holds two words: blue cosmos. Wide, like a fresh sketchbook, with far more still undrawn than drawn. We are filling it in, one square at a time.

Tonight there is another drawing on the kitchen table. The next game will probably start there.
