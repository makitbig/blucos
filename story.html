Our story

Games made by a kid who draws and a dad who codes

Our kid draws on an iPad. Since they were little, the drawings have piled up into the thousands. Each new one got shown around the family, and we always liked looking.

For a long time, Dad felt those drawings deserved more than a photo library. Games, stickers, something other people could enjoy too. Blucos started from that thought. The drawings and ideas come from our kid, and Dad turns them into code, one game at a time.

Old favourites, plus collecting

The games themselves grew out of puzzles Dad loved growing up: bingo, minesweeper, Hexa, 2048. To each one we added the fun of collecting. You hatch birds, dig up fossils, light up constellations and follow evolution, and the birds, fossils and stars are all real.

Something to play together

Our family sits around the table reading, drawing and playing games. We would like these games to fit into time like that: something that brings back old games for mums and dads, and something kids can play right beside them.

To be honest, we don't know yet whether kids today will love them. What we are sure of is that they should be games a parent can hand over without worrying. That is why there are no ads between levels and nothing that asks you to pay for a chance.

About us

We are a small family studio on Jeju Island, and we make one game at a time. The name Blucos comes from blue cosmos.
