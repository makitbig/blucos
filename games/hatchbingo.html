Hatch Bingo

Hatch chicks in three lines and fill your bird book

Coming soon

Tap a tile to grow it and the tiles above, below, left and right by one stage: egg, cracking egg, chick, and egg again. Fill three lines (rows, columns or diagonals) with chicks and the big egg hatches into a real bird.

Screenshots

What you collect

  • 20 real birds across five habitats: City & Garden, Forest, Waterside, Grassland and Polar
  • Every bird you hatch goes into the Bird Book, with a card and a real fact
  • From level 25 a cuckoo hides among the eggs: its tile stops changing, and a line through it can't be completed

The Aviary

  • The birds you hatch grow up in the Aviary in real time
  • Come back the next day to see how they have grown

Play every day

  • 100 boards, from easy to tricky
  • The Daily Egg brings coins every day
  • Hint, Undo and +3 taps, bought with coins earned in the game or with an optional video

Fair and friendly

  • Coins and items can't be bought with money; nothing you buy depends on luck
  • No ads between boards: only a small banner in the free version, and videos only when you choose them
  • Plays offline, in 10 languages

Hatch Bingo Pro (one-time purchase)

  • Tropics, Rare birds, Lost birds and Dinosaur kin packs: 16 more birds and 80 new boards
  • No banner ad; Hint, Undo and +3 taps without videos
  • Double coins and one of each item every day
