Petal Garden

Match petals, grow real flowers and send them as cards

Coming soon

A gentle match-three puzzle where every flower bed you finish helps a real flower grow. Swap petals to make lines of three or more, clear the weeds and melt the frost, and watch each flower grow over three beds, from a small plant to full bloom.

Screenshots

Real flowers from world gardens

  • 360 beds in eight gardens: an English cottage garden, Dutch tulip fields, a Japanese garden, a Mediterranean hillside, desert blooms, a tropical garden, an alpine meadow and an East Asian garden
  • 120 real flowers, painted by hand, each with its Victorian flower meaning and a true story
  • Seasons that follow your own hemisphere

Send a flower

  • Send any flower you have grown as a card: choose the paper and lettering and write your own message
  • Good-morning cards, and birthday cards with the flower of each birth month
  • Water your garden once a day and watch it shimmer

At your own pace

  • No timers, no lives, no pressure
  • Plays offline, in 10 languages

Petal Garden Pro (one-time purchase)

  • No banner ads, double coins and a daily gift of each helper
  • All four Pro gardens; they can also be opened one by one with coins earned by playing
