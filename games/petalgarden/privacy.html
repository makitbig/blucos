Privacy Policy

Petal Garden · Effective November 28, 2026

English · 한국어

The short version

  • No accounts. Your garden and progress stay on your device. Only when you play side by side do your score and name pass briefly through our server, and they are deleted within 10 minutes.
  • Ads in the free version are served by Google AdMob, which may use device identifiers.
  • We do not run our own analytics.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

1. Data stored on your device

Your beds, bloomed flowers, coins, items, card designs and messages, birth month, settings and language are stored only in the app's storage on your device. The season shown in your garden is worked out on your device from its time zone and language; nothing is sent to us. We cannot see any of this, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

2. Flower cards

Flower cards, with any message you write, are drawn on your device. When you send one, it goes through your phone's own share sheet to the app or person you choose; we never receive it. Saving a card to your photos needs your permission, which you can change in your device settings.

3. Playing side by side

When you play together with people next to you (Play together, side by side), the players' phones show each other's petals during the race and the ranking at the end. To do that, the app sends our server at api.blucos.studio (run on Cloudflare) the match number, a random key made new for each match, your petal count and the name you typed, if any. The key is not a device ID and is not linked to you. A room is deleted 10 minutes after its last update; we keep no device IDs, no accounts and no request logs. Without an internet connection, each person plays on their own and nothing is sent. Challenge links you send to friends do not use our server.

4. Analytics and crash reports

The app does not send analytics or crash reports to us. If we ever add them, we will update this policy before the version that includes them is released.

5. Advertising

The free version shows a small banner at the bottom of the screen and, only when you choose to watch one, rewarded videos from Google AdMob. There are no ads between boards. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect:

  • device identifiers, such as the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, advertising ID on Android), if you allow it;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • ad interactions, such as which ads were shown or tapped;
  • diagnostic data, such as ad loading errors and performance.

Your consent. On iOS the app asks whether to allow tracking. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not based on your activity in other companies' apps and websites. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choices before ads are shown; you can change them at any time from Menu › Ad privacy. You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings.

Buying Pro removes the banner, doubles the coins you earn, gives you one of each item every day and opens the four Pro gardens. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

6. Purchases

Petal Garden Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. The app checks on your device whether Pro has been purchased; we never receive your name, Apple or Google account, payment card or billing details. Coins and items cannot be bought with money. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.

7. The list of other Blucos games

To keep the "More games from Blucos" list in the menu up to date, the app downloads a small public file (the names, pictures and store links of our games) from GitHub Pages once per launch. Nothing about you or your play is sent with it; like any website, GitHub receives your IP address to deliver the file.

8. Email

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply, and delete them when the conversation is over.

9. Children

Petal Garden is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has contacted us, email us and we will delete the information.

10. Your choices

  • Turn tracking off at any time in your device's privacy settings (iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking).
  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, review your ad choices from Menu › Ad privacy.
  • Delete all game data by deleting the app.
  • Ask us any question about your data at the address below.

11. Changes and contact

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.
