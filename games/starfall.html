Starfall: Star Match Puzzle

Match falling stars and light up real constellations

Coming soon

Starfall is a calm, colorful puzzle where every stage is a real star. Drop groups of three stars, line up three or more of the same color, and set off chains. Win a stage and that star lights up in its constellation, in its true color, with its real distance from Earth.

Screenshots

Constellation journey

  • 18 constellations across the four seasons: Orion, the Big Dipper, Scorpius, Cassiopeia and more
  • Every star is a stage, and star colors follow real star temperatures: blue is hottest, red is coolest
  • Finish a constellation to watch its lines appear and read its story
  • Special stages based on real facts: Algol's eclipses dim the board, Mizar's double stars count twice, meteor showers rain down, and the Seven Sisters hide in the Pleiades

Learn the real sky

  • Tonight's Sky shows where your constellations are right now from your city
  • My Night Sky collects everything you have lit on one star map
  • The Constellation Book keeps every myth, fact and star

Friendly for everyone

  • Row Comet, Column Comet and Black Hole items, earned by playing
  • Classic mode that gets faster level by level
  • Plays offline, in 10 languages, with no interstitial ads, ever

Starfall Pro (one-time purchase)

  • No banner ads; continue and preview the next pieces without watching videos
  • Southern Sky pack: the Southern Cross, Centaurus with Alpha Centauri, Carina with Canopus, and the Southern Triangle
  • Eastern Sky pack: the Northern Dipper with its old star names, and the Four Symbols of the 28 lunar mansions

Real data

  • Star positions come from the Yale Bright Star Catalog; distances and facts were checked against current sources
