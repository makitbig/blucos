Fossil Dig: Dino Museum

Dig up fossils and fill your own dinosaur museum

App StoreGoogle Play coming soon

Grab your pickaxe and become a paleontologist. Every dig site hides fossil bones under the soil. The numbers on dug squares tell you how many rocks are nearby, so you can work out where it is safe to dig. Hit a rock and your pickaxe takes a knock; three knocks and the dig is over.

Screenshots

What you collect

  • 17 real prehistoric animals: Tyrannosaurus, Triceratops, Stegosaurus, Velociraptor, Pteranodon, the woolly mammoth and more
  • Each dig uncovers one part: skull, body, legs or tail. Find all four and the skeleton comes together, with a painting and a fun fact
  • Medals for completing animals again in harder seasons, and golden fossils

Build your museum

  • Every finished skeleton goes on display in your own museum
  • Visitors come to see your collection and pay for tickets: the more you show, the more they come
  • The floor plan shows which hall is the most popular

Dig around the world

  • Real fossil sites: Western North America, the Gobi Desert, the Jurassic seas of Europe, the Western Interior Seaway and the Ice Age lands
  • Each site has seasons with more rocks and more bones
  • A Daily Dig with the same ground for everyone
  • New sites open with dig permits bought with the coins your museum earns

Fair and friendly

  • Every board can be solved by logic, no guessing needed
  • No ads between digs, ever: only a small banner on the dig screen in the free version, and videos only when you choose them
  • Coins are earned in the game and never sold; nothing you buy depends on luck
  • Plays completely offline

Fossil Dig Pro (one-time purchase)

  • Three more dig sites right away: sea reptiles, pterosaurs and Ice Age giants
  • No banner ad, rewards without watching ads, and a coin gift every day
