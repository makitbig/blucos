Evolution 2048: Merge Puzzle

Merge matching tiles and follow the story of evolution

Coming soon

The 2048 you love, where every tile is alive. Swipe to slide the board. When two of the same tiles meet, they merge and evolve: bacteria become jellyfish, fish crawl onto land, dinosaurs rule, and finally a human appears on the 2048 tile. Every new stage comes with a quick fun fact.

Three free themes

  • Evolution: from bacteria to humans
  • Gems: from plain quartz to a dazzling diamond
  • Classic: pure numbers for 2048 purists

Make it yours

  • Create your own themes with your photos, emoji and names
  • Turn your family, your pets or your favorite snacks into an evolution chain
  • Photos are cropped right in the app and never leave your phone

Play your way

  • Board sizes from a tough 3x3 to a relaxing 6x6 marathon
  • A new Daily Challenge every day: the same board for everyone, with a streak to keep
  • Evolution items: Mutate a tile, wipe one out with a Meteor, or mix up the board with a Twister

Fair and friendly

  • No ads between games, ever: just a small banner in the free version
  • Optional videos only when you choose them, to refill undos or items
  • Plays completely offline
  • 17 badges and lifetime stats

Evolution 2048 Pro (one-time purchase)

  • Pro Packs: Space, Fruits, Animals, Vehicles, City and To the Moon, with more on the way
  • Customize every stage of your themes
  • Unlimited undo and no banner ad
