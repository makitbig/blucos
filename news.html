Our first game is out in the world

On November 17, 2026, Fossil Dig: Dino Museum arrived on the App Store. It is the very first game Blucos has released.

We won't forget the moment we pressed Release. A little world that had only ever moved in our two pairs of hands is now moving in someone else's, somewhere we may never see.

Fossil Dig is a logic puzzle: the numbers tell you how many rocks are nearby, and you dig around them. Uncover the bones of 17 real prehistoric animals, from T. rex to the woolly mammoth, complete each skeleton and put it on show in your own museum. Every dig can be solved by reasoning alone, never by guessing.

The Google Play version is on its way, and our other games, about birds, stars and evolution, are waiting their turn.

If you find something fun down there, or get stuck, write to us. The first letters about a first game are ones we will keep for a long time.
