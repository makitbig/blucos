For parents

Good to know before you hand it over

Ads, purchases, privacy: the things parents usually ask about. Every game we make follows the same rules.

Ads: one small banner

The free versions have one small banner at the bottom of the screen. There are no full-screen ads when a level ends.

Video ads play only when a player chooses one, for a hint, an undo or another try. You can finish every game without watching any.

No paying for chance, one payment

There are no loot boxes, gacha or other paid chance. Coins and items are earned by playing and never sold for money.

Each game has one thing to buy: Pro, paid once. It removes the banner and opens extra packs. No subscriptions, no automatic renewals. Apple and Google handle the payment, so Screen Time and Family Link purchase limits still apply.

Personal information kept to a minimum

No accounts, no sign-in, no servers of our own. We don't ask for a name or an age, and progress is saved only on the device. Photos used in a custom theme stay on the device.

Some games use Google Firebase to receive anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, so we can see which parts are played and fix problems. They never include names or anything typed.

Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may use device information to show them. Each game's privacy policy, linked below, has the details.

No internet needed

Every game can be played to the end without an internet connection.

Real birds, fossils and stars

The birds, prehistoric animals and constellations in the games are all real. Completing one opens a short note about it, which can be nice to read together.
