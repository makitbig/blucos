For parents

What's inside, before you hand over the phone

Before you hand a phone to your child, you want to know what is inside. We are a family raising a child too, so we understand. Here it is, plainly.

Ads stay quiet, and stay at the edge

The free versions have one small banner at the bottom of the screen. We don't show ads that cover the screen when a level ends; we never want to break a child's concentration just as it gets going.

Video ads play only when your child wants a hint, an undo or another try, and taps to watch one themselves. Skip them all, and the whole game is still there to finish.

No paying for a chance

No loot boxes, no gacha, no mystery chests. Coins and items in our games are earned by playing and are never sold for money.

Each game has exactly one thing to buy: Pro, paid once. It removes the banner and opens new packs. No subscriptions, nothing that renews every month. Apple and Google handle the payment, so any purchase limits you have set with Screen Time or Family Link still apply.

Your child's world stays on your child's device

No accounts, no sign-in, no servers of our own. We never ask for a name or an age, and progress is saved only on the device. Photos used in a custom theme never leave the phone.

The ads come from Google AdMob, which may use information about the device to show them. Each game's privacy policy explains this in full.

No Wi-Fi needed

Every game plays to the very end offline: on long car rides, on planes, at grandma's house in the countryside.

The conversation after the game

The 20 birds, 17 prehistoric animals and 18 constellations in our games are all real, and each one your child completes opens a short, true story.

So when the game is over, ask: which bird hatched today? Which constellation lit up? If your child starts telling you and can't stop, our game has done its job.
